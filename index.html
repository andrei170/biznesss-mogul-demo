What people actually ask.

Who is Sheikera Coutain?

+

Sheikera "Ginger" Coutain is an MBA, PMP, Six Sigma Black Belt, and DEI-certified leader with two decades of corporate experience. She is the founder of Ginger LuxeReal — a luxury Florida real estate practice that delivered a 150% sales lift — and The Biznesss Mogul ™, an executive mentorship brand serving women of color pivoting from corporate to ownership.

What is The Biznesss Mogul?

+

The Biznesss Mogul ™ is Sheikera's mentorship and content brand for women of color making the executive-to-entrepreneur pivot. Sessions cover strategy, wealth building, leadership, and legacy planning. Application-based intake with a small annual cohort.

What does Ginger LuxeReal do?

+

Ginger LuxeReal is Sheikera's luxury real estate practice in Florida — Palm Beach, Bal Harbour, Fort Lauderdale, Fisher Island, Miami, Jupiter, and Parkland. Buyer and seller representation, portfolio strategy, and the bespoke client experience that drove a 150% lift in luxury sales.

Margate, Florida — with active deals across South Florida luxury submarkets and an international consulting footprint that traces back to her two decades in corporate strategy.

She accepts a small number of executive mentorship clients each year, prioritizing women of color making serious moves out of corporate. Inquiries via this site or directly through The Biznesss Mogul Instagram.
